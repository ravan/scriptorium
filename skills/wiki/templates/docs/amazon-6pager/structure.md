# Amazon 6-pager structure

A narrative memo for a decision meeting: the room reads it in silence, then discusses. Prose carries the argument - **no bullets anywhere** (the renderer refuses them for this template). Complete sentences force complete thoughts.

Skeleton:

1. **Introduction** - what decision this memo asks for, in the first paragraph.
2. **Goals** - what success looks like, measurable where the wiki has numbers.
3. **Tenets** - the 3-5 principles that guided the proposal; a reader who accepts the tenets should find the plan inevitable.
4. **State of the business** - where things stand today, honestly, including what is not working.
5. **Lessons learned** - what past attempts taught; this section earns trust.
6. **The plan** - the proposal itself, with costs, risks and the decision explicitly stated.
7. **FAQ** - the hard questions a skeptical reader would ask, answered directly. This is often the longest and most-read section; do not soften the questions.

Rules of the form:

- Hard cap ~3300 words of body (six pages); the FAQ may continue as an appendix beyond it if genuinely needed.
- Data belongs in sentences ("we shipped 14 of 20 planned workbooks") or in an appendix figure - never in bullets.
- No product marketing tone; the memo assumes an internal, skeptical reader.
- Callouts are not part of this form; do not use them.
